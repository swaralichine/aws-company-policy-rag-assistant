ExampleCo — Arizona Remote Work Policy
Policy ID: RW-AZ-001
Version: 1
Effective date: November 1, 2026
Location: Arizona

Arizona employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week.

Manager approval is required before choosing a remote work schedule.

Core collaboration hours are 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM Arizona local time.

Remote work outside the United States requires separate written
approval from Human Resources.

Equipment reimbursement is covered by a separate policy.
