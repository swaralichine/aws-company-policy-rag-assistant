ExampleCo — California Remote Work Policy
Policy ID: RW-CA-001
Version: 2
Effective date: November 1, 2026
Location: California

California employees may work remotely up to 4 days per week.

Manager approval is required before choosing a remote work schedule.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Pacific Time.

Remote work outside the United States requires separate written
approval from Human Resources.

Equipment reimbursement is covered by a separate policy.
