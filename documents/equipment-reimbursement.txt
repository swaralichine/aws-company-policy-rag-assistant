ExampleCo — Equipment Reimbursement Policy
Policy ID: EQ-001
Version: 1
Effective date: November 1, 2026
Location: United States

Eligible employees may claim up to $300 per calendar year for
approved home-office equipment.

Manager approval is required before purchasing equipment.

Requests must include an itemized receipt and be submitted within
30 days of purchase.

Unused allowance does not carry over to the next year.

Internet service charges are not covered.
